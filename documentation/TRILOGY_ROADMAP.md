## Trilogy Arc

**Paper I** establishes existence and uniqueness of the Coralia set C under axioms C1–C9.

**Paper II** demonstrates that these axioms have empirical content—they predict where mathematical constants land when rounded to integers.

**Paper III** completes the classification by proving axiom independence and ceiling necessity, establishing that the axiom system is minimal.
