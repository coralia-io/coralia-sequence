# Scope

The Coralia Sequence is **not** a universal law. It is a finite ratio family that recurs in bounded, discretized systems under constraint.

## What This Is

A 12-element integer sequence with a specific gap structure:
- **C** = {0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12, 15, 23, 30, 35}
- **Gaps** = [1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 8, 7, 5]
- **4 zones** with ascending regularity, terminal Fibonacci-Lucas cascade
- **Unique** under 9 axioms (proven via enumeration)

## What This Is Not

- Not a theory of everything
- Not a universal pattern for all systems
- Not applicable to unbounded or continuous domains
- Not a replacement for domain-specific models

## Where It Applies

The Coralia structure appears in systems that share:
1. **Discretization** - finite, countable states
2. **Boundary constraints** - defined limits on range
3. **Phase transitions** - qualitative regime changes
4. **Rhythm or periodicity** - temporal or spatial recurrence

## The 43% Cliff

The position 15/35 = 3/7 ≈ 0.4286 marks a regime change - the transition from ascending regularity (zones 1-3) to descending cascade (zone 4). This is not a magic number; it's a structural property of systems with this ratio family.
